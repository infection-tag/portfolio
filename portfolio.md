Welcome to my portfolio. Here are a few projects I have been working on.

## [Flights](/flights.md)
* **Technologies:** Vue.js. three.js, globe.gl
* **Description:** Analyzed prices of flights in May 2026, and noted the various global and domestic issues that have culminated in flight costs at the time.

## [War Games and Dot Products](/wgdp.md)
* **Technologies:** MATLAB, Linear Algebra, Python
* **Description:** Created a neural network made for analyzing war, and used singular value decomposition to find what factors the neural network prioritized. 

## [HinderD Distribution Network](/hinderd.md)
* **Technologies:** Python, Node.js
* **Key Achievement:** Distributed over 200 doses of life-saving Narcan to underserved areas in the Bay
* **Description:** Created a heatmap in Node.Js with publicly available overdose data to find which areas in San Francisco were not being served with Narcan in time.
